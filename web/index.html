Method

Annual values from NASA POWER monthly point data (MERRA-2 reanalysis), 1991–2025. Trend by Mann–Kendall (two-sided, tie-corrected) and Sen's slope, computed in ingest.py. Multiple comparisons controlled with Benjamini–Hochberg. A significant trend is evidence of a change, not an explanation of one: reanalysis grid cells are modelled estimates, not thermometers, and 35 years is short for separating a trend from a multi-decade cycle.

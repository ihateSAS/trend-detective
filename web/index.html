Four beats, in order.

  1. /01

    (A striking trend)

    Dhaka cooled −0.23 °C per decade (p = 0.012) while its rainfall rose +24.7 % (p < 0.001).

    (Eighty tests)

    Thirty-four look significant. Thirty survive Benjamini–Hochberg at q = 0.05.

  3. /03

    (Did we pick the places?)

    Named sites give ρ = −0.55. A grid nobody chose gives −0.05. The pattern was in our sample.

    (Report the audit)

    Not the headline. The tool below shows both, side by side, on one chart.

(Method & credits)/05©2026

(Method)

Annual values from NASA POWER monthly point data (MERRA-2 reanalysis), 1991–2025. Trend by Mann–Kendall (two-sided, tie-corrected) and Sen's slope, computed in ingest.py. Multiple comparisons controlled with Benjamini–Hochberg. A significant trend is evidence of a change, not an explanation of one: reanalysis grid cells are modelled estimates, not thermometers, and 35 years is short for separating a trend from a multi-decade cycle.

(Data & credits)

NASA POWER monthly point API (MERRA-2, community AG) · T2M, PRECTOTCORR. Map outline: Natural Earth 1:110m (public domain). Methods: Mann (1945); Kendall (1975); Sen (1968); Benjamini & Hochberg (1995).
